CuNNy DS 2x upscalers as ncnn, in the sizes faster, fast, 4x12, 4x16, 4x24, 4x32 and 8x32 (cunny-<size>-ds-rp.param and cunny-<size>-ds-rp.bin)

Based on CuNNy DS, Copyright (c) 2024 funnyplanter, https://github.com/funnyplanter/CuNNy

Modified on 2026-11-11 (8x32) and 2026-11-15 (the other sizes): converted from mpv/ds/CuNNy-<size>-DS.glsl (commit e18bcf2cb4f6efb4f28be9ebb5c3fd03c1dfff0d) to ncnn. Same weights; added an RGB wrapper (BT.709 luma, bicubic chroma) and edge replication before every convolution.
